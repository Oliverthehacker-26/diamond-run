Diamond Run

You start in your base. Drive out with your crew, find 3 diamonds across the huge map, then return to the base and seal the world.

Fighter shoots zombies. Healer slowly heals the crew. Cook makes meals that boost your speed. Dangers: dark nights, fast zombies, brutes, short fuel, spike strips and a car that can break (the cook repairs it for 1 food). Each diamond slows you down and calls a horde. If you run dry you walk, and zombies slow down.

Choose a level. 1 Easy, 2 Normal, 3 Hard, 4 Nightmare (little fuel, swarms of the dead). Level 5 is a boss battle: an ice dragon follows you everywhere. Dodge its frost breath, and lure it into your base cannons to bring it down.
